About关于我

I am an industry researcher at Skywork AI. My work focuses on the post-training of large language models, particularly reinforcement learning for reasoning, reward modeling, and learning from human preferences.

我目前在Skywork AI从事大语言模型研究，关注模型后训练，尤其是面向推理的强化学习、奖励建模与人类偏好学习。

My research spans reinforcement learning for reasoning (DAPO and Skywork-OR1), reward modeling (the Skywork-Reward series), software engineering agents (Skywork-SWE), and multimodal generation (SkyReels-V4). Earlier, I worked on question answering and machine reading comprehension.

我的研究涉及推理强化学习（DAPO、Skywork-OR1）、奖励建模（Skywork-Reward 系列）、软件工程智能体（Skywork-SWE）和多模态生成（SkyReels-V4）。早期工作聚焦问答与机器阅读理解。
