About关于我

I am an industry researcher at Skywork, Kunlun. My work focuses on the post-training of large language models, particularly reinforcement learning for reasoning, reward modeling, and learning from human preferences.

我目前在昆仑万维 Skywork从事大语言模型研究，关注模型后训练，尤其是面向推理的强化学习、奖励建模与人类偏好学习。

I have contributed to the Skywork-OR1, Skywork-Reward, Skywork-Reward-V2, and Skywork-SWE projects. Across these efforts, I am interested in how training data, reward signals, and optimization shape model capabilities.

我参与了 Skywork-OR1、Skywork-Reward、Skywork-Reward-V2 与 Skywork-SWE 等项目，关注训练数据、奖励信号和优化方法如何影响模型能力。
